Lab Switcher — placement

Sits directly under the LabOS brand mark at the very top of the sidebar — visible on every screen, one click away regardless of which page you're on. It opens a dropdown listing every lab the signed-in staff member belongs to, each row showing the lab's name, code, and their role there, with a checkmark on the currently active lab. Switching reloads the workspace scoped to that lab; nothing else in the sidebar or header needs to move.
